Our Philosophy

Cooking that speaks in one clear voice

Lumière keeps eighteen seats and one nightly menu. Nothing is on the plate that doesn't need to be — each dish is edited down until only the essential flavor remains.

We work directly with a handful of growers and fishmongers within a day's drive, so the menu changes as the season does, not on a fixed schedule.

"The best table in the room is the one where nothing calls attention to itself but the food."
